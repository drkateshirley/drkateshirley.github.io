Clinical Practice

Health challenges can affect many aspects of life, sometimes making longstanding concerns such as anxiety, depression, or difficult relationship patterns harder to manage. I am interested in understanding these experiences within the larger context of each person's life.

Psychotherapy

My approach is collaborative and grounded in evidence-based practices. I draw from cognitive-behavioral, Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), psychodynamic, and relational approaches, depending on each person's needs and goals.

Our work might involve taking time to better understand what you're experiencing, exploring patterns that have been difficult to change, or finding practical ways to manage current challenges. I value understanding your experiences and what matters most to you.

Psychological Assessment

I provide psychological evaluations to clarify diagnoses, better understand emotional and cognitive functioning, and inform treatment planning. My assessment approach draws on clinical interviewing, standardized measures, and consideration of the broader medical and psychosocial context.

Areas of assessment include

  • Diagnostic clarification
  • Adult ADHD
  • Mood, anxiety, and personality functioning
  • Cognitive screening and assessment*
  • Psychological aspects of chronic illness and other health conditions
  • Treatment planning

*I am not a neuropsychologist and do not provide comprehensive neuropsychological evaluations. Individuals requiring comprehensive neuropsychological testing will be referred to an appropriately trained specialist.
