Psychological Assessment

I provide evidence-based psychological evaluations to clarify diagnoses, understand cognitive and emotional functioning, and guide individualized treatment recommendations.
Areas of assessment include: Diagnostic clarification Adult ADHD Mood, anxiety, and personality functioning Cognitive screening and assessment* Psychological aspects of medical and neurological conditions Treatment planning *Please note that I am not a neuropsychologist and do not provide comprehensive neuropsychological evaluations. Individuals requiring comprehensive neuropsychological testing will be referred to an appropriately trained specialist.
Assessment services anticipated beginning October 2026.I am a clinician-scientist who examines access to and engagement with evidence-based psychological interventions within integrated healthcare systems, with particular attention to cognition, substance use, and the structural conditions that shape who receives care and when. I work at the intersection of intervention science, implementation research, and health systems inquiry — bringing direct clinical experience into the design and interpretation of empirical work.
