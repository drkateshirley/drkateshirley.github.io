Research

My research examines cognitive functioning and cognitive rehabilitation among adults with substance use disorders, traumatic brain injury, and co-occurring psychiatric conditions. I am particularly interested in developing and evaluating interventions that address cognitive difficulties, understanding how cognitive functioning influences treatment engagement and everyday functioning, and improving access to evidence-based care for underserved populations, including rural Veterans.

My work draws on clinical trials, intervention development, implementation science, and health services research. I am a member of the Brain Health Research and Informatics (BHRAIN) Lab at Oregon Health & Science University and maintain research collaborations with Brown University and the Providence VA Medical Center.
