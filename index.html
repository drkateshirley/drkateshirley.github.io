Clinical Practice

I provide evidence-based psychotherapy and psychological assessment for adults navigating the psychological challenges of medical conditions, chronic illness, anxiety, depression, and life transitions. My goal is to help you navigate life's inevitable challenges with greater flexibility, self-understanding, and purpose. I anticipate opening my private practice in October 2026. I am not currently accepting clients. Please check back for updates regarding availability.

Research

I am a clinician-scientist examining access to and engagement with evidence-based psychological interventions within integrated healthcare systems, with particular attention to cognition, substance use, and the structural conditions that shape who receives care and when.
